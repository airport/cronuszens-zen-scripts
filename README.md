# Cronus Zens: free Cronus Zen scripts

Free GPC scripts for the Cronus Zen, published by [Cronus Zens](https://cronuszens.com).

Cronus Zens (cronuszens.com) is an independent community that publishes GPC scripts for the Cronus Zen. It is not affiliated with Collective Minds, who make the Cronus Zen.

## Scripts

| Script | Game | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Shot Tuner](shot-tuner/) | NBA 2K | Takes your jumpshot on the right stick with a Timing and Tempo you set on the Zen |

More free scripts for NBA 2K27, Fortnite, Call of Duty, Rainbow Six Siege, Rust and ARC Raiders are listed at [cronuszens.com/scripts](https://cronuszens.com/scripts).

## Put a script on your Zen

You do not need Collective Minds' Zen Studio desktop app. Open [cronuszens.com](https://cronuszens.com) in Chrome, Edge or Opera on Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS, plug your Zen's PROG port into the computer and press Connect your Zen. Drag a `.gpc` file onto a slot and press Program.

Step by step: [How to program scripts](https://cronuszens.com/guides/program-scripts).

# Shot Tuner

A free NBA 2K shot script for the Cronus Zen by [Cronus Zens](https://cronuszens.com).

Press Square (X on Xbox) and Shot Tuner takes the shot on the right stick: it holds the stick down for your Timing, then flicks it up over your Tempo and holds it at the top. The Square press itself never reaches the game while Shot Tuner is on.

## Settings

| Setting | Range | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Shot Tuner | On or off | On |
| Timing | 100 to 1500 ms | 600 ms |
| Tempo | 5 to 200 ms | 98 ms |
| Reset | Turns Shot Tuner on and puts Timing and Tempo back to default | |

Hold L2 and press OPTIONS (LT and Menu on Xbox) to open the menu on the Zen's screen. Up and Down pick a row. Left and Right change Timing or Tempo by 1 ms, or by 10 ms while you hold R2 (RT). Cross (A) turns Shot Tuner on or off on the first row and runs Reset on the last. Circle (B) closes the menu. Your settings are saved on the Zen.

## Install

Program [shot-tuner.gpc](shot-tuner.gpc) to a slot from [cronuszens.com](https://cronuszens.com) in Chrome, Edge or Opera, no Zen Studio needed. Steps: [How to program scripts](https://cronuszens.com/guides/program-scripts).
